Mogriva

Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Data collection

Mogriva does not collect personal data from use of the app. It does not require a Mogriva account, use advertising trackers or analytics, or upload your projects or media to a Mogriva server. We do not sell personal data.

Projects and media

Your drafts, imported media, and editing preferences are stored on your device. Mogriva uses the files and media you choose for editing. If you share a project or export, you choose the recipient or destination, and that service’s privacy policy may apply. You can delete projects in the app. Removing the app also removes its local app data. Files you saved elsewhere are managed separately.

Purchases

Apple processes all Mogriva Pro purchases through the App Store. Mogriva uses Apple’s StoreKit to verify Pro access and restore purchases on your device. Mogriva does not receive or store your payment card details or Apple Account credentials. Apple processes purchase and billing information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

This website and support

These pages use no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts them and handles web requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you contact us, we use the information you choose to share to respond.
